Hey! I'm Jay — a BSIT student who enjoys building websites, experimenting with designs, coding random ideas, and turning small concepts into something people can actually use.

🐾 What went into this?

Meow Page started as a simple idea: make sending a message feel a little more personal than just sending a chat.

Inspired by Green Day's “Last Time on Earth” and its postcard imagery, I wanted to turn that idea into a little digital postcard — something you can write, decorate, and send to someone special. 💌

So I turned it into a tiny digital postcard — with cats, stamps, themes, music, animations, and shareable links. 💌
