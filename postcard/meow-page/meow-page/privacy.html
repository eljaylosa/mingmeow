Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. 💌 This Privacy Policy explains how Meow Page handles the information you provide when creating and sharing a postcard.

📋 What We Collect

When you create a postcard, you may provide:

  • Recipient name
  • Sender name
  • Postcard message
  • Uploaded stamp/image
  • Selected postcard theme
  • Other information you voluntarily enter

🔐 How Your Data Is Protected

Your postcard data is stored using Supabase, which provides security features for data storage and transmission. Connections to the website use HTTPS to help protect information while it is being transmitted.

Access to stored data is also controlled through database security rules where applicable.

⚠️ Important

Meow Page does not claim that your messages are end-to-end encrypted. Please avoid entering passwords, financial information, private credentials, or other highly sensitive information into postcards.

🔗 Shared Postcards

When you generate a shareable postcard link, the postcard data may be retrieved by anyone who has access to that link.

Do not include sensitive or confidential information in a postcard that you intend to share publicly.

🖼️ Uploaded Images

If you upload a stamp image, the image may be stored as part of your postcard so that it can be displayed when the postcard is opened through its shareable link.

🚫 What We Don't Need

  • Passwords
  • Credit/debit card information
  • Government IDs
  • Private account credentials
  • Highly sensitive personal information

🗑️ Data Removal

If you need a postcard or its associated data removed, please contact the person responsible for maintaining Meow Page and provide the postcard's shareable link or identifier.

📌 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Meow Page's features and data-handling practices change.

By using Meow Page, you acknowledge that you understand how your postcard information is handled as described above.
